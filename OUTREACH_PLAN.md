# AI Business Assistant — First Customer Outreach Plan

This document outlines a practical, low-friction outreach strategy for securing the first real pilot customer for the **AI Business Assistant**.

---

## 🎯 1. Target Customer Segment Analysis

### **Primary Target Segment: Local Coaching Institutes & Technical Training Centers**

#### **Why This Segment?**
1. **High Inquiry Volume**: Coaching institutes and skill academies receive daily repetitive inquiries regarding course lists, fee structures, batch timings, class modes (online vs. classroom), and locations.
2. **High Lead Value**: Converting a single student inquiry can yield significant revenue for the institute, making lead capture highly valuable.
3. **Owner Accessibility**: Local training centers are typically owned and managed directly by the founder or director, allowing direct communication without enterprise gatekeepers.
4. **No Complex IT Dependencies**: Coaching centers rarely require deep legacy ERP integrations; a standalone web assistant and lead dashboard fits their exact workflow.
5. **Staff Availability Gap**: Reception staff are frequently busy during peak class hours or offline during evenings/weekends when prospective students research courses.

---

## 💡 2. The Problem We Solve & Value Proposition

- **The Problem**: When prospective students visit a coaching institute's website or social page after hours, they cannot get instant answers about course fees or batch schedules. If staff are unavailable, the student leaves and contacts a competing institute.
- **Our Honest Value Proposition**: 
  > *"We help local training institutes capture prospective student inquiries 24/7 by adding an intelligent assistant to their website that answers course and fee questions accurately and collects callback details for your team."*

## 🔄 4. Follow-Up & Objection Handling Scripts

### **A. Follow-Up for Non-Responders (Send 3 days after initial message)**

> *"Hi [Name], just following up briefly! I know you're busy running [Business Name]. Even if you aren’t looking for software right now, I’d welcome 2 minutes of feedback on our prototype. Let me know if you’d like to see a quick 2-minute video overview. Thanks!"*

---

### **B. When Interested in a Demo**

> *"Awesome! Thanks [Name]. I can show you the live system in just 5–10 minutes over Google Meet or Zoom. Would [Day] at [Time] or [Alternative Day/Time] work better for you?"*

---

### **C. Objection: "We already have a website / social media page."**

> *"That's great! Our assistant actually embeds directly onto your existing website or landing page as a small widget. It turns your passive site visitors into active leads by answering their specific questions immediately and asking for their contact number so your team can call them back."*

---

### **D. Objection: "We already use a WhatsApp auto-responder / basic chatbot."**

> *"That makes total sense. Basic auto-responders are great for static menus, but they can't handle natural follow-up questions (like comparing course durations or specific fee details). Our assistant understands natural conversation and records every lead cleanly into an admin dashboard for your team to manage."*

---

### **E. Response to Pricing Questions: "How much does it cost?"**

> *"Right now, we are offering a 14-day risk-free pilot where we configure the assistant with your exact course list and fees for free. If you love it and want to keep it running after the trial, our standard plan is [e.g. $29–$49/month], with zero setup fees or long-term contracts. Would you be open to trying the free pilot first?"*

## 💬 5. Discovery Question Script

When speaking with a business owner, use these 4 discovery questions to uncover their pain points:

1. *"How do prospective students currently inquire about your courses when your office is closed or busy?"*
2. *"What are the top 3 questions your front-desk staff spend the most time answering every day?"*
3. *"Approximately how many website visitors or social media leads leave without giving their phone number?"*
4. *"How does your team currently track which leads have been called back and which ones enrolled?"*

---

## 🚀 6. Low-Risk 14-Day Pilot Offer Structure

- **What the Business Receives**:
  - Custom AI Web Chatbot configured with their course catalog, pricing, working hours, and FAQs.
  - Dedicated Admin Lead Dashboard for tracking student callback requests.
  - 14 Days of free usage during the pilot period.
- **What We Need from the Business**:
  - A text/PDF copy of their course list, fee structure, batch schedules, and common FAQs.
- **Disclosed Limitations**:
  - The assistant operates strictly on configured business facts (no invented policies).
  - During the initial pilot, the demo assistant runs on a managed host with fallback support.
- **Success Metrics**:
  - Total student inquiries answered.
  - Number of callback lead phone numbers captured.
  - Front-desk staff time saved.
